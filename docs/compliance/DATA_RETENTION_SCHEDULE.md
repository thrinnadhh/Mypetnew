# Data retention schedule

Status: engineering policy and production gate, 2026-08-15. Periods are defaults, not assertions that a statute always requires the full period. Legal must approve tax, consumer, pharmacy and litigation periods before production. A legal hold must be documented, scoped and reviewed; it must not silently preserve every data class.

## Enforcement model

1. A daily lifecycle job should select expired rows by data class, write an audit result without copying personal content, and alert on failure. That job is not implemented; production release is blocked until it is.
2. Deletion first disables identity and sessions, revokes device tokens, deletes cart and direct profile identifiers, deletes Customer-owned pet/address rows, withdraws active consent, and writes a tombstone. Retained order/loyalty references require a documented legal basis and later pseudonymisation/purge.
3. Backups are not an indefinite exception. Restore procedures must reapply every active tombstone before serving traffic and record proof. The present 35-day suppression window is an engineering assumption that must match the selected provider's verified backup window.
4. Retention changes require privacy, security and legal review, a migration/job update, and updated evidence in the compliance matrix.
